# Master Data change and Pay proposals

# Segregation of Duties

### Segregation of duty policy for Multi mainly focus of separation of work between maintaining master data (supplier / tenant information) vs. payment process with arguing that 4 eyes principle should be always in and persons who has right to add / amend critical information should not be involve payment process to avoid possible fraud. 

![segrigationofduties_v2.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Master%20Data%20change%20and%20Pay%20proposals/segrigationofduties_v2.jpg)

## **Master data change**

### Right to create and amend supplier data in coda. E.g. amending iban number, address, payee etc. (can be done over country wide only)

## **Approval of master data change**

### Someone who has right to approve changes made my someone. Else. E.g. accountant made changes iban number of PwC NL and Acccounting Manager needs to approve it via coda workflow to make it live (to make payment) again. (can be done over country wide only). On top of this creation and amending changes subject ot approval of legal department in perspective of KYC control

## **Creation of Pay Proposal**

### This role will have right to create / amend payment proposal over, due and approved invoice via coda workflow. This person should not have access to maintain master data. In practice this is cash accountant or treasurer. (can be done over legal entity level as well)

## **Approval of Pay proposal**

### This person will be who has right to approve and process payment orders according the power of attorney. Most cases country FD and MD at initial. Based on limit this will go thru Company board and BX where needed.  (can be done over legal entity level as well)
