# Closing general information

### Below you can find general information for the quarterly close regarding deadlines and intercompany interest.

## **Closing deadlines **

### The quarterly closing deadline is the 5th working day after the end of the quarter.
If the closing date will be different this will be communicated by the HQ accounting team.

## **Intercompany Interest**

### Intercompany interest charged in the intercompany accounts:

  | Rate | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2019 ** | **4,60%** | ** 4,60% ** | ** 4,60% ** | **4,60% ** |
| **2020 ** | **4,60%** | ** 4,60%** | **4,60%** | 4,60% |
