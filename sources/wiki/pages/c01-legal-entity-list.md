# C01 Legal entity list

## Control C01 Legal entity list

Control C01 Legal entity list shows an overview of all entities (EL1) per Coda company / country.
Per entity various details of the entities are shown.
These settings have impact on reporting, so if details don't reconcile please contact the HQ accounting team.
