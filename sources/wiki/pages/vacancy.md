# Vacancy

## **Definition**

### A unit is considered vacant when

- **the unit is not occupied by a tenant or otherwise in use, and
**
- **it can be leased out to another tenant**

### As soon as the unit is occupied / in use but we don’t receive ‐or receive less‐ leasing income, we report it as [discount](/sites/Wiki/Paginas/Discounts.aspx) and not as vacancy.

## **Rule of thumb**

- **When a unit is vacant, we report it as Vacant. No other reporting lines
**
- **We do not reverse ERV or PBR to zero but report Vacancy instead to ultimately report a Base Rent that equals the Cash received
**
- **As soon as the unit is occupied / in use but we don’t receive / receive less leasing income, we report it as Discount**

## **Case1**

### In 2017, Management of the shopping center is deliberately not leasing out a specific unit. The reason is that in two months time, the unit next to it is becoming vacant and Management can sign a three year lease with another tenant for the combined sqm of the two units. Over 2018 and 2019 the lease contract is signed, and the contract amounts to EUR 90. ERV of the vacant sqm is EUR 100

![Vacancy_01.PNG](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Vacancy/Vacancy_1.PNG)
**Rule of thumb**

- **PBR equals the full contract rent, not deducting for the vacancy
**
- **We do not adjust ERV or PBR because a unit is vacant
**
- **The gap between PBR and actual Cash received is reported as Vacancy
**
- **Base Rent reflects the actual Cash received
**
- **We show a GRI that equals the actual Cash received**

## **Case2**

### At the beginning of the year, the unit is vacant. At the end of Q2 we sign a lease for EUR 80 (annualized) with the contract starting in Q3. ERV is EUR 120. We assume PBR is equal to ERV for Q1 and Q2.

![Vacancy_01.PNG](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Vacancy/Vacancy_2.PNG)

## **Rule of thumb:**

- **PBR equals the full contract rent, not deducting for the vacancy
**
- **We change the PBR (via the Rev Potentional line) per the starting date of the (new) lease agreement
**
- **The gap between PBR and actual Cash received is reported as Vacancy
**
- **Base Rent reflects the actual Cash received
**
- **We show a GRI that equals the actual Cash received**

### **Note**

### Also when the contract is signed in Q1 but only commences starting Q3, we do not change the PBR for Q2. Instead we report Vacancy that is equal to ERV. We do not change PBR as we may find a temporary tenant / pop‐up store for Q2 only.
