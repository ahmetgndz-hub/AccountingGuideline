# Partly Turnover Rent Contract

# **Partly Turnover Rent Contract**

## **(with fixed component / minimum rent)**
A rental contract where the leasing income partly depends on the retail income of the tenant.

## Case

We close a contract by which the tenant pays a rent amounting to 10% of its turnover rent, with a minimum guaranteed rent of EUR 60.  Tenants sale over 2017, 2018 and 2019 amounts to EUR 500, EUR 550 and EUR 700. ERV of the unit is EUR 100

![to rent1.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/turnover-rent/to%20rent1.jpg)

- We report PBR at the level of the fixed (minimum) amount

- Base Rent reflects the Cash received, excl any excess per the turnover dependent terms agreed in the lease contract

- The excess income is reported as Turnover Rent

- We show a GRI that equals the actual Cash received
