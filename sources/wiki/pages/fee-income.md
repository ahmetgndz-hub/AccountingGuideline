# Fee Income

# Fee Income (applicaple for service companies)

### As main income category for service companies is management fee income in multi service companies and split up in 4 different catagories. 

- **Asset Management fee
**
- **Property management fee
**
- **Statutory service fee
**
- **(re) Development fee
**

## **New Accounts for 3rd party fee Income**

### Fee income from 3rd party malls will be booked under these accounts to report on income properly. Below accounts only valid for management companies which generates "management fee income". You are expected to reclass all 2018 income to these accounts which generated from 3rd party malls (if any)

- **Reporting on country file will remain in same reporting category as group income.
**
