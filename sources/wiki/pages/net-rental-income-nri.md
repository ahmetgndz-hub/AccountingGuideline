# Net Rental Income - NRI

## Definition

“Net Rental Income” or “NRI” means all revenue from the Asset(s) less operating expenses including service and marketing costs that are allocated to the tenants. Net Rental Income includes base rent invoiced to the tenants and other income from the Asset(s) like parking income, rent from turn-over rents and services and marketing charges charged to the tenants. Net Rental Income is calculated before tax, amortization and interest payments, financing costs, depreciation, valuation results, general expenses and income tax provisions.
