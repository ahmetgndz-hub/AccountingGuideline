# C16 AR matching control

# C16 AR matching control

This is a tool to control for matching on Account receivable.
The reporting is showing the outstanding amounts at the account receivables where at a specific el1 and el6 combination is at least one credit amount.
An unmatched credit amount indicates that there might be a possibility that this amount can be matched with an outstanding invoice (debit).
