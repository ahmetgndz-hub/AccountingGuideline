# OCR (Occupancy cost ratio or Effort Index)

## **Definition**

The effort index (trailing 12 months) is calculated as follows:

![ocr.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/ocr-(occupancy-cost-ratio-or-effort-index)/ocr.jpg) 

- Not included non-sales reporting tenants.
- Include tenants that have at least sales of 12 consecutive months at the period of measurement
- Exclude shop consumption utilities and special units, like gas stations
- Not include turnover from mall income:

  - Kiosk
  - Temporary stands,
  - ATMs;
  - Antennas;
  - Temporary Storage
