# Booking Structure

# Using different set of books

### Multi choses CODA Finance as ERP and one of advantage is using multiple set of books to facilitate different reporting needs in one set of financials. MAN books (on element3 level) is basis for everything and below table shows how we approach different set of books next to MAN books.

![books.png](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Booking%20Structure/Booking%20Structure-01.jpg) 

## **MAN: Shareholder reporting and management decision making (Follow cash as much as possible)**

- **Basis for all reporting of all Multi and BX owned companies (Multi Group Accounting Policies)
**
- **Used for management & shareholder reporting, decision making, cash flow forecasting, distribution process etc.**
- **Investment Property at BX Investment Value**
- **Rent Free and Stepped Rent incentives directly in the P&L **
- **Tenant Fit-out and Direct Cash Contribution incentive capitalization subsequently in the P&L through Market Value Changes **
- **Loan Arrangement Fees directly in the P&L
**

## **FSM: Multi IFRS consolidated financial statements**

- **Basis for the Multi Group IFRS consolidated financial statements
**
- **Refer to separate overview of the accounting adjustments**
- **Investment Property at Multi Internal Valuation**
- **Lease Incentives and Loan Arrangement Fees are straight lined based on the remaining term of the lease / loan contract**

## **FSE: Financial statements to the banks**

- **Basis for the IFRS financial statements that we submit to the Banks**
- **Refer to separate overview of the accounting adjustments**
- **Investment Property at the Valuation as used for reporting to the Bank (external appraiser, basis for LTV calculations)**
- **Lease Incentives and Loan Arrangement Fees are straight lined based on the remaining term of the lease / loan contract** 

## **LOC: Statutory financial statements that comply with local GAAP**

## **TAX: Containing the numbers used in the income tax filing**

## **UST: To capture specific adjustments to US Tax Packs **

## **LUX: To bridge the gap, when applicable, to Luxemburg GAAP**
