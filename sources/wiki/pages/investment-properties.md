# Investment properties

### The fair value of the investment property should be measured at the reporting date at the amount that would be received at that date from the sale of the property in an orderly transaction. 
**Capital expenditures should be reflected directly to profit or loss accounts** under financial statement line "loss on fair value of investment property" unless these expenditures cause increase in capacity. 
For clarification all capital expenditures should be **initially booked under investment property** with element5 CXX codes. Other codes link to IP account will be blocked. 

- **C1 – Regular Capex                              Expense
**
- **C2 – Legal Compliance                         Expense
**
- **C3 – Redevelopment                             Capitalize
**
- **C4 – Tenant fit out / Incentives              Expense
**
- **C5 – Extensions                                    Capitalize
**

### As yields and unobservable inputs are unchanged, fair value of investment property should be same as at the last valuation. (unless C3 and C5 has value). 
Fair values communicated by HQ should always be round with using following excel formula. 

### ROUNDDOWN(D5,-4)  from 12,356,936 = > 12,350,000, from 16,999,897 =>  16,990,000 etc.

### 2 valuations are calculated BX and Multi valuation, optional there is a third party valuation. 
The BX and Multi value are shared by HQ.

| **Valuation owner ** | **Fequercy ** | **EL3 ** |
|---|---|---|
| **BX Valuation ** | **Quarterly ** | **MAN ** |
| **Multi Valuation ** | **Yearly (Q4) ** | **MAN + FSM ** |
| **External ** | **when needed ** | **MAN + FSE ** |

### * in the LOC books the local value can be presented (for example purchase price, straightlining incentives etc)

### Redevelopment (C3) and exentensions (C5) are taken in to account in the BX and Multi Valuation. 
After processing the valuation in the books the value should be presented as follows: 
Books: IP (11111) + WIP (11340) = valuation

### The Multi value only needs to be processed in the FSM books if the property is Multi owned or on special request.
For BX properties there is no need to process the Multi value in the FSM books.
