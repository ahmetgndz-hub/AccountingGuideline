###  SCoA to book dividend payable liability towards shareholders has been created to serve below NRI reporting as well as better classification of liabilities in balance sheet.

|---|---|
| **Management Hierarchy mapping** | **BF4 Trade and other payables group companies** |
| **Purpose of use ** | **This SCoA to be used to record dividend payable towards its shareholder. First payable amount, minus its taxes minus legal reserves needs to be stored under equity and net payable will be booked to this SCoA. ** |
