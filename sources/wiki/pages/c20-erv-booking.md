# C20 ERV booking

### for proper presentation of reversionary potential, it has been decided to book ERV at asset level and we would like to make sure that amounts reflected to books and budget in line with horizon. For this reason, we make some control over assets and these are listed below.

| **A - Full year ERV (stored in Horizon) ** | **Data source is horizon and picked up all ERV figures starting from reporting period ** |
|---|---|
| **B - Full year ERV (budgeted)** | **Data source is coda budget for related reporting period** |
| **C - (A-B) 1st control point** | **This fields compares data stored in horizon as ERV for full year vs budgeted in business plan period.** |
| **D - Year to Date ERV booked ** | **booking made to account 44101 (ERV) year to date** |
| **E- Year to date ERV reversal** | **booking made to account 41111 (ERV reversal) year to date** |
| **F - Year to date TO rent only ** | **booking made to account 41416 (Turnover rent only) account year to date** |
| **G - Calculated field** | **Column A divided by 12 months times reporting period to find expected amount from ERV stored in horizon** |
| **H - Calculated field** | **Column B divided by 12 months times reporting period to find expected amount from erv budget stored in Coda** |
| **I - (G+D) 2nd control point** | **comparing expected data from horizon vs booked (C is full year compare whereas this is YTD compare)** |
| **J - (H+D) 3rd control point ** | **comparing budgeted vs booked in actuals** |
| **K - (D+E+F) 4th control point** | **comparing ERV + ERV reversal + TO rent only. only difference allowed here is agreed rent - erv. ** |
