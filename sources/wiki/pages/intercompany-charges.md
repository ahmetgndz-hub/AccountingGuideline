# CAF

## **Recharge HQ > SCM**

## **Agreement / CAA - Cost Allocation Agreement**

### HQ performs management, advisory, functional guidance and assistance and centralized services for the benefit of the SCM.

### The organization costs of HQ, adjusted for shareholders costs (-/-) and mark-up (+/+), are pro-rata charged to the SCM with an annual minimum of Eur 250.000.

### This fee does not include the direct costs charged out under the ACAA (HQ)

# PMSF

# PLSF

## **Recharge SCM > SPV**

## **Agreement / PLSA - Property, Leasing & Services Agreement**

### SCM provides management services to the Asset Companies (SPV). Management services consist of property management, leasing management and statutory services.

### Annual fee is based on a percentage of the Net Rental Income increased with actual staff costs of on-site employees

### Split recoverable Property Management (PM) versus non-recoverable Asset management (AM) and Statutory Services (SS): 65% resp. 20% and 15% as a general guideline (could vary per specific asset/country).

# CAF IGS

## **Recharge SCM > SCM**

## **Agreement / IGSA - Intra Group Service Agreement**

### The charge is based on the pro-rata part of the direct staff costs increased with pro-rata part of the indirect costs that can be allocated to the supplied services increased with a mark-up.
