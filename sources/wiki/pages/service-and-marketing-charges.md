# Service and marketing charges

## **Recoverable service charges (BO Codes involved PE – PF)**

### As part of managing properties there are some cost incurred which are agreed to be re-charged to the corresponding tennants. For this reason, Multi has 2 BO reporting group as follows.

## **PE – Service and marketing charge income**

### This account group will be used to record service and marketing advances from tenants which we collect from tenants to funding expenses behalf of tenants. However, these advances might have few clauses according the lease contracts. E.g. cap, floor, fixed etc.

![PE-F.png](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Service%20and%20marketing%20charges/Service%20and%20Marketing_01.PNG) 

### CAP            Tenant expenditure share capped with an amount for a maximum. E.g. total tenant share is 500 EUR for period however tenant capped this amount up to 400 eur. In this case 100 eur will be cost of landlord.

### Fixed          In this case tenant pays fixed amount for service / marketing charge expenditures and loss / gain stemming from this operation stays with landlord.

### Floor          Even rare situation sometimes tenants contribution limited with lowest amount and tenants still liable to pay more than this amount. 

###                   This can be seen as reverse side of CAP and most times used in connection with CAP. 

## **PF – Service and Marketing charge expenses**

### Expenses incurred for recoverable service and marketing charges (in line with lease agreement and local legislation) expected to be booked under this reporting group with proper account.
