# Annual sales per sqm

The annual sales per sqm can be calculated in two different ways:

## Total annual sales / SQM

Total annual sales should be calculated as follows:

![Sales1.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Annual%20Sales/sales%20per%20sqm/Sales1.jpg) 

- Not include storage areas.
- The above should not include turnover & GLA from Mall income:

  - Kiosk
  - Temporary stands,
  - ATMs;
  - Antennas;
  - Temporary Storage

## Like for Like sales per sqm (trailing 12 months)

Like for like sales per sqm is calculated as follows:

![sales2.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Annual%20Sales/sales%20per%20sqm/sales2.jpg)
 

- Comparable sales reporting tenants is defined as a sub-selection of tenants that have been reporting sales for the last 24 consecutive months (to enable year on year comparison on a LfL basis)
- Storages areas should be excluded from the GLA used in the denominator (as they are not generating any Sales)
- Including only "Comparable" units and excluding "Mall income".
