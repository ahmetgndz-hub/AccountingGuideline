# C12 Valuation

### Horizon keeps track of all valuations related to the property. In this exercise, we compare valuation data from the horizon (sol position) vs actual booking from coda (IST position) and compare them and see if we have differences vs agreed valuation on the specific moments for different types of books. 

![2020-07-03_11-22-43.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/C12%20Valuation/2020-07-03_11-22-43.jpg)

- **The first block (first 3 columns of below table) shows data related asset**
- **2nd block (columns 4, 5, and 6) shows differences between SOL and IST position. **
- **3rd block (7-11 columns) shows the latest known valuation of an asset from the horizon with corresponding date next to it. If the date is at the reporting period then highlighted with green.**
- **4th block (last 4 columns) shows data from coda which has been booked.**
