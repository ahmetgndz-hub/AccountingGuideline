# C26 Booking after closing deadline

# C26 Bookings after deadline

Bookings made to MAN and FSM books after closing deadline.

For more information on the closing deadlines see the following link: [Closing general information](/sites/Wiki/Paginas/Closing%20general%20information.aspx)
Please always communicate when the closing deadline for some reason cannot be met and communicate the bookings to be done after deadline to HQ accounting.
