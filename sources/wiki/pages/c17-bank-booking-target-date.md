# C17 Bank booking target date

### This is not a control but kind of measurement to keep light of quality and timely bookkeeping of bank statements which is crucial to have up to date information any time. 

### Expected bank bookkeeping target date is next working day after bank statement. for this purpose, PQ created to calculate targeted bank bookkeeping date (bank statement date + 1 working date) e.g. bank statement of Monday needs to be booked at Tuesday. Bank statement of Friday needs to be booked at Monday till end of day. 

### This targeted bank bookkeeping date compared with actual bookkeeping date (input date from coda) and difference in days grouped in 4 buckets as on time, within 4 days and after 4 days. Please note date bank holidays has not been considered in this calculation. 

### At below example first line

### 1139 bank statements booked at q1 at expected timeframe

### 119 documents are booked in next 4 days

### only 4 documents booked after 4 days. 

### Average is stated as 1,16 which is quite good and 1 is ultimate level where we would like to be.

### maximum is just giving sense what is maximum days stay without booking bank statement. (That may present even 1 document)
