# C11 Dummy and suspense accounts

This is a tool to check the usage of the suspense accounts and dummy element 6.

All suspense accounts should be empty and matched every single day. Only one exception is possible; transfer from account A at day 0 and the funds arrive at account B at day 1. In this single exception it is possible to have an unmatched amount available at a suspense account. Suspense accounts should be checked and solved every day.

The usage of dummy element 6 is not allowed. Dummy element 6 are for example, but not limited to: 0, xxxDUMMY9, Cxx99999, Dxx99999, any other element 6 named dummy, technical, temporary etc. Only exception is the use of element 6 "0" for depreciation booking and transfer of result from P&L to equity.
