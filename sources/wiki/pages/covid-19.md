# Covid-19

### During the Covid-19 pandemic various actions will be taken to support our assets, tenants and service entities.
On this page we will update these actions with explanation of the impact on accounting. 

**Assets**

### **Temporary Covid-19 discount**

### To support our tenants during Covid-19 it is possible to grant the tenants a temporary extra discount on the monthly invoices.
This temporary extra discount needs to be processed on the following SCoA:

### BO Code:             PC2 'Short term discounts'
 SCoA:                  41410 'Operational discounts'
Element 5:           Specific 'Covid-19' element 5 to be created per country (to be requested via Servicedesk/Coda-team/Horizon team)

![discount.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Covid-19/discount.jpg) 

## **Service entities**

### **Government scheme's **

### As a response to Covid-19 governments make job retention scheme's or other scheme's available for companies.
For example, the government will support in paying the salaries. The company will pay the employees for 100%, but can recover a percentage of this salary from the government.

### This will be a temporary income, to not influence the regular costs or income we will use the following SCoA to book this income from the government:

### BO Code:             PT 'Other operating income'
 SCoA:                  47520 'Other income (Man.Co)'
Element 5:           Specific 'Covid-19' element 5 to be created per country (to be requested via Servicedesk/Coda-team)

### Don't forget to map this new Element 4/5 combination to your local hierarchy, to avoid unmapped items in your local reports.
