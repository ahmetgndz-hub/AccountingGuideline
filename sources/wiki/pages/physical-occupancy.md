# Physical Occupancy

## Definition

Physical occupancy refers to actual number of units have leased out. Calculation should be as follows.

 ![physical occupancy.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/physical-occupancy/physical%20occupancy.jpg)

- Included storage areas (storage which are linked to a specific lease and long-term (>1 year))
- The physical occupancy **should not include**:

  - Kiosk

  - Temporary stands,
  - ATMs;
  - Antennas;
  - Temporary Storage

- Measures the extent to which the leasable space within a mall is fully occupied based on sqm potential (rather than rent foregone as measured by financial occupancy)
