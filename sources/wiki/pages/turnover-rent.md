# Turnover Rent

### Within Multi we have different type of treatments for turnover rent as follows. 

## **Case 1 Tenants who pays turnover with performance based on top of base rent.**

### In this case lease agreements consist of

- **base rent as well as
**
- **turnover rent  percentage on top of base rent**

### These tenants called "performance TO tenants" and below example shows how to treat them in reporting as well as in bookkeeping. 

| ![TO case1 reporting_v3.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Turnover%20rent/Turnover%20Rent_01.PNG) | ![TO case1 booking_v3.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Turnover%20rent/TO%20case1%20booking_v3.jpg) |
|---|---|

**Case 2 Tenants pays turnover rent only "temporarily basis"**

### In this case tenants lease agreement "switched" to "Turnover rent only" at certain moment to support tenant financially and original lease agreement contains base rent. However Multi waved this base rent for specific time frame. 

| ![TO case2 reporting_v3.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Turnover%20rent/Turnover%20Rent_02.PNG) | ![TO case2 booking_v3.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Turnover%20rent/TO%20case2%20booking_v3.jpg) |
|---|---|

## **Case 3 Tenants pays turnover rent only as per day 1 of lease agreement**

### In this case lease agreements consist only turnover rent  percentage "without" base rent component

### These tenants called "TO rent only tenants" and below example shows how to treat them in reporting as well as in bookkeeping.

### In this example short term discount can be positive or negative based on sales capability of tenant.
