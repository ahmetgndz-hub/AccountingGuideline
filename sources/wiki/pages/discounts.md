# Discounts

| **Stepped RentsCondition, agreed in the Master Lease Agreement, that the rent level of a tenant grows gradually over a multiple years period.  **Case**We close a 3‐year rental contact with a tenant, and agree in the Master Lease Agreement that the rent per year gradually grows towards EUR 80 in the last year. Over 2017 the tenant pays EUR 60, over 2018 EUR 70 and over 2019 EUR 80. ERV of the unit is EUR 100. ** |

| **Operational (temporary) DiscountsCondition, agreed in an Amendment to a Master Lease Agreement, to temporarily (**<1 year**) lower the rent level during a specific time frame.**Case**We have a 3‐year rental contact of EUR 80 as agreed in a Master Leasing Agreement (‘ MLA’). However afterwards, to support the tenant, we agree on a temporary rent reduction in an Amendment to the MLA. We agree on a discount, in the first year only, of EUR 30. ERV of the unit is EUR 100. ** |

| **Structural Discounts (during the course of the contract)CaseFor a specific unit in the shopping center, we have set the ERV at EUR 100 and the PBR at EUR 90. Master Lease Agreement includes a 3‐year rent level of EUR 90. However, at the end of year 1 we close an Amendment on the Agreement, and agree on a structural decrease of the annual rent to EUR 80. ** • **This is **not considered** a lease incentive but permanent commercial price adjustment ** • **The fact that we are not able to close a lease agreement for the ERV set for a long time, will be an input for setting the ERV going forward, but that should **not** drive adjusting the ERV in the current period**MAN and IFRS Books![41412Structual discounts.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Discounts/Discounts%207.PNG) • **PBR equals the full contract rent, excl the structural discount** • **We do not adjust the ERV or PBR** • **The (permanent) discount provided is reported as Discounts** • **We report a Base Rent that equal the Cash received ** • **We use scoa 41412 Operational Discounts ** |
| **Discounts during a Fit-Out Phase CaseWe closed a lease agreement with a tenant late Q1, effective Q2 of EUR 80 (annualized). The tenant performs fit out work (at his own expense) which takes one month in Q2. During the fit out phase a part of the unit is not operational. After closing the Master Lease Agreement, the tenant asks us for a compensation of loss in his retail income. In answer to his request, we grant the tenant with a short term discount of EUR 10. During Q1, the unit was vacant. ERV is set at 100.** • **This is **not considered** a lease incentive but a compensation of lower ability to generate shop turnover to the tenant. It is not a condition to be able to close or renew a leasing agreement. Thus, not considered an incentive and not capitalized. **MAN and IFRS Books![41420 Fitout.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Discounts/Discounts%208.PNG) • **PBR equals the full contract rent, excl the discount ** • **We do not adjust the ERV or PBR ** • **The discount provided is reported as Discounts ** • **We report a Base Rent that equal the Cash received ** • **We use SCoA [41420](/sites/Wiki/Paginas/41420.aspx) Fit out phase** |
