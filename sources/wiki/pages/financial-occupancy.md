# Financial Occupancy

## Definition 

This is the average occupancy rate of the portfolio over the year calculated on the basis of ERV at the reporting date, as a percentage of the theoretical rental revenue.

![financial occupancy.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/financial-occupancy/financial%20occupancy.jpg)

- Include storage areas (storage which are linked to a specific lease and long-term)
- The above **should not include**:

  - Kiosk
  - Temporary stands,
  - ATMs;
  - Antennas;
  - Temporary Storage

- Vacant units are values against ERV
