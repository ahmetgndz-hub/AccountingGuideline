# Full Turnover Rent Contract

| Full Turnover Rent Contract(without fixed component / no minimum rent) A rental contract where the leasing income entirely depends on the retail income of the tenant. **Case ** We close a contract by which the tenant pays a rent amounting to 10% of its turnover rent, with no minimum guaranteed rent. Tenants sale over 2017, 2018 and 2019 amounts to EUR 500, EUR 550 and EUR 700. ERV of the unit is EUR 100 ![torentpnl2.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/full-turnover-rent-contract/torentpnl2.jpg) • We report PBR of EUR 100 same ERV level • Base Rent reflects the Cash received, excl any excess from the turnover contract so EUR 0 • The excess income is reported as Turnover Rent • We show a GRI that equals the actual Cash received ![torentbooking2a.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/full-turnover-rent-contract/torentbooking2a.jpg)  Example 2 Example asset has 5 units with following conditions ![20191015 TO Rent only -1.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Full%20Turnover%20Rent%20Contract/20191015%20TO%20Rent%20only%20-1.jpg) Final Layoout of P&L will be as follows ![20191015 TO Rent only -2.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Full%20Turnover%20Rent%20Contract/20191015%20TO%20Rent%20only%20-2.jpg) Expected bookkeping will be as follows. ![20191015 TO Rent only -3a.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Full%20Turnover%20Rent%20Contract/20191015%20TO%20Rent%20only%20-3a.jpg) Some other examples![2019-10-17_13-36-57.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Full%20Turnover%20Rent%20Contract/2019-10-17_13-36-57.jpg) ![2019-10-17_13-38-13.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Full%20Turnover%20Rent%20Contract/2019-10-17_13-38-13.jpg) ![2019-10-17_13-35-54.jpg](/sites/Wiki/PublishingImages/Paginas/Full%20Turnover%20Rent%20Contract/2019-10-17_13-35-54.jpg) |
