# Available budget calculation

For our Purchase Request Module in Coda we make use of the available budget calculation.
Below the logic of the available budget calculation is described.

The available budget calculation facilitates:

- Budgets with a budget period not equal to regular year (for example 3rd party assets with a budget year from April to March);
- Actuals from previous year won't influence the current year budget, if approved Purchase Request from previous budget year is available;
- Accruals are taken into account in the calculation;
- The date of the Purchase Request is determining the budget period used for the available budget calculation.
- Budget calculation level is determined on group level.
