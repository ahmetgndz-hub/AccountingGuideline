# Non recurring expenses

Dear All

 

Please be informed that below new SCoA 's issued as per 1st of September.

 

**New Accounts for 3rd party fee Income**

Fee income from 3rd party malls will be booked under these accounts to report on income properly. Below accounts only valid for management companies which generates "management fee income". You are expected to reclass all 2018 income to these accounts which generated from 3rd party malls (if any)

 

- To Do for countries :  please contact with FAM team to make proper element5 mappings to these accounts and make reclasses if necessary.

 

| **SCoA** | **Name** | **Element1 group** | **BO Code** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **45811** | Asset Man Fee income 3rd | Non asset | PP1 |
| **45821** | Property Man. Fee Income 3rd | Non asset | PP2 |
| **45831** | Statutory service fee income 3rd | Non asset | PP3 |
| **45881** | Development management fee inc. 3rd | Non asset | PP4 |

 

 

**New Accounts for non-recurring costs**

As management information there is a need to report our expenses in split with recurring and non-recurring expenses. So for this information collected from countries manually. To decrease workload on country and HQ we have decided to create 3 more  SCoA to classify only "non-recurring" expenses. Mainly non-recurring expenses incurred in below 3 categories.

 

- To do for countries :  please contact with FAM team to make proper element5 mappings to these accounts and make reclasses if necessary.

 

| **SCoA** | **Name** | **BO code** | **Element6 gr** | **Element7 gr** | **Element8 gr** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **44126** | Staff cost (non-recurring) | PQ5 | Employee | Department | n/a |
| **44594** | Advisory (non-recurring) | PR12 | 6CRE, 6GRP, 6REL, 6CD | 7DEP, I*, 0 | Gen, ovh |
| **44596** | Other non-recurring cost | PR12 | 6CRE, 6GRP, 6REL, 6CD | 7DEP, I*, 0 | Gen, ovh |

 

 

Besides that transactions on following "existing" SCoA 's will be deemed as non-recurring expense.

 

| **SCoA** | **Name** | **BO code** |
|---|---|---|
| **44125** | Redundancy (staff) | PQ5 |
| **44591** | Restructuring Costs (general) | PR12 |
