# C21 Bank booking without Crescendo

Bookings done on cash accounts (excluding 13332 and 13312) without use of Crescendo.
Please check for these bookings/bank accounts whether there is a possibility to have the bank statements downloaded from the bank portal and then uploaded to CODA via Crescendo instead of booking on CODA itself.
