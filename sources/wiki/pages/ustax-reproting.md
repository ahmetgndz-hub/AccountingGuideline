# USTax reproting

- **Building and Improvements** (BS-TANCO-BUIMP) will be reported as purchase price as you shared with us without adding any capex.
- **Capital Expenditures **during period will be released to valuation result (PL-DEPAM-REVAL) at reporting date.
- **Capital expenditures for (re) development** will be classified as "Buildings & Improvements - under Construction" till finalization.
- Besides that capital expenditures will be reported as separate tab in reporting pack to separate

  - Valuation effect
  - Capex incurred during period

And this has to be reconcile with amount posted to (PL-DEPAM-REVAL) valuation result.
