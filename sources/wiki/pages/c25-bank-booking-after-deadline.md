# C25 Bank booking after deadline

# C25 Bank booking after deadline

These transactions are booked after cash bank booking deadline which is set always 3 working days after period end and communicated by the cash manager.
When the deadline of 3 working days need to be exceeded for some reason, please contact the cash manager/HQ accounting.
