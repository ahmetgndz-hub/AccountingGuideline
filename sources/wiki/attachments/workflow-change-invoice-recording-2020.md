Dear all,

**Intro**

During the past reporting cycles, we were confronted with variances resulting from accounting treatments which were not in line with our accounting manual. To further improve the quality of the accounting input, we will adjust the invoice workflow. The country head of accounting will be added to confirm proper accounting of all invoices entered by the country accounting team.

**Scope**

Journal vouchers were already approved by the country head of accounting. We are now extending the input check to invoices as well. The intention is to make sure invoices are recorded correctly the first time (no need for inefficient corrections) and in line with the Multi accounting manual. Heads of accounting are expected to check at least:

- document date within reporting period;
- SCoA used in line with cost;
- VAT correctly reflected (reimbursable or not);
- counterparty correct;
- amounts on the invoice correct;
- description correct and in English;
- attachment correct;
- Etc.

**Timing**

Starting next week.
