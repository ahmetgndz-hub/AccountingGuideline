Dear all,

Herewith are the instructions for the October closing of the Multi Asset, Legacy, and Service companies. Considering that you may have different deadlines for 3rd party mandates, our deadlines might be treated as guidance for the 3rd party asset companies, as it will enable you to close the Service companies properly. For accurate reporting and an efficient process, countries must complete the below tasks within the deadline (earlier is, of course, preferred).

The **14th of November 2024** is the deadline for October 2024 closing! Access to coda will be closed centrally on the 15th of October. If you anticipate any delays in completing the closing, please let us know as soon as practical.

The** quality of closing prcedures**, is being evidenced by the status of the control files. The FD review of control files and trial balances, evidenced by sign-off by FD, is to ensure that nothing has come to the FDs attention that causes him to believe that the financials do not give a the true and fair view of financial position and results for the period. This precdure is to ensures that control files are either clean or that issues have been flagged, explained and will be resolved as soon as practical.

The map below highlights the country's performance on control files for September. Despite improvements made, the colour coding reveals that significant opportunities for improvement for red and orange-shaded countries still exist.

To enhance efficiency, please use the Financial Performance Review (FPR) file to explain any material variances between actuals and budget. This proactive approach will help us avoid the need for follow-up calls to discuss budget differences.

Thanks in advance, and success with the closing!

Kind regards,

Ahmet Gunduz
