1. GENERAL BUDGET INSTRUCTIONS 2026
Preparation principle: The basis for the Budgeted Fee Income of the Service Companies is the Budget of the underlying real estate portfolio managed by Multi.
Guidelines for Budgeting
- Present in local currency
- Give an estimate of the EURO FX rates per quarter in 2026
- Only include new business to the extent signed
- Fee income is based on the 2026 NOI, Service Charges and Capex 2026, Collections, etc as agreed with the Property Owner
- Outline your (re) letting fee assumptions (for instance, frictional vacancy, vacancy management, structural vacancy, etc)
- HQ will provide Official CPI country inflation % for forecasting Income and Cost
- HQ will provide each country with the CAF assumptions 2026 per quarter
- The Staff and Genex budgeted recharge needs to be reconciled within the group before final submission of the 2026 Budget. HQ will coordinate the reconciliation process
Guidelines for Budgeting (continued)
- Please use the IC Charges File detailing the charges per country (as shared by Ahmet recently). HQ will signal when the charges between countries are final for you to include in your Budget 2026
- Gross Margin per asset is calculated as Fee income -/- Staff cost direct employees -/- Indirect Staff and Genex (see separate slide)
- Do not anticipate on asset disposals, unless the Owner provided exclusivity
Scope
- Include Actuals 2024, Budget 2025, Forecast 2025 and Budget 2026 (refer to Annex 1)

2. REQUIRED LEVEL OF DETAIL
Service Company
- Fee income: Fees are budgeted at the level of the asset (el2), type of fees (el4), detailed description of the fee (el5) and client (el6). Elements are required for the Forecasted File you will be submitting
- Staff costs: Separate process between MD's and Multi Board. The Salaries 2026 and Bonuses 2025 as proposed to the Board, should be in line with your submitted Budget'26. Forecasting file only at SCOA level (el4), not at employee level
- Genex: Coda upload file at SCOA level (el4), and counterparty code (el6)
For the RMA Presentation, please use the format as attached (not prior versions!)
In the Margin table, the Indirect staff cost and Genex are allocated to mandates based on the average of
- % of leases (number of leases of the applicable mandate, divided by the total number of leases managed)
- % of fee income (fee income from the applicable mandate, divided by the country's total fee income)
An example calculation is included in Annex 1 – Template Result from Management Activities

3. STAFF COSTS BUDGET - FTE OVERVIEW, SALARIES, BONUS
Preparation of the Staff costs budget is a separate process between MD's and Multi Board
Guidelines on the assumptions/budget
- Please include an FTE overview, split into direct and indirect staff, per yearend 2025 and per yearend 2026
- New hires to include based on Board approval, unless already approved (and paid for) by the 3rd party Owner
- Budgeted Redundancy cost will be provided by HQ
- Assume recharges of Staff cost to the Assets whenever permitted under the management contract

4. TIMETABLE
13 Oct 2025: Instruction Budget 2026 to countries
24 Oct 2025: HQ shares assumptions on inflation, FX, GDP, CAF and Genex recharges
14 Nov 2025: Country to submit Draft Budget 2026, incl the Salary file and Bonus proposal
17-28 Nov 2025: Comments and questions on Budget 2026
17-28 Nov 2025: Meetings Board with MD/FDs
5 Dec 2025: Country to submit Final Budget 2026 based on approved Salaries 2026
9 Dec 2025: Consolidated budget review meeting Board
12 Dec 2025: Final consolidated and country budgets

ANNEX 1 – TEMPLATE RESULT FROM MANAGEMENT ACTIVITIES
Notes on the Time / cost allocations
1. Time charges between countries: When an employee on the payroll of country A incurs time for country B. Part of Staff Costs. Example: a Finance Director on the payroll of Multi Germany performs the same role for Multi Swiss
2. Genex cost charges: Cost that are contracted by the country itself are shown on the respective Office cost, ICT cost, Advisory cost, Insurance cost, etc. line. Cost that are recharged by HQ to other countries, like Coda/Horizon/BO licences, are shown on the 'Costs recharged from/to group companies' line
3. Time charges HQ <-> countries: When an employee of HQ incurs time for country A, to the extent not already charged under 1) above. Example: HQ Legal manager reviews an employment contract for Poland or HQ Accounting manager supports in the closing of the books
