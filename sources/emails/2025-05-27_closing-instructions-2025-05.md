Dear all,

Herewith are the instructions for the May closing of the Multi Service and Multi/3rd Party owned Asset companies. Knowing you may have different deadlines for 3rd party reporting, our deadlines can be treated as guidance for the 3rd party reporting, as it will enable you to close your Service companies.

The below planning should be respected and the closing steps completed, so we can do a proper and timely communication of the May results to the Board

**Planning**

For May 2025, the closing deadlineis set at** 13 June 2025.** Access to Coda will be closed centrally on the 14th of June. If you anticipate any delays in completion, let us know as soon as possible.

**Closing Steps**

The minimal set of closing procedures is listed below. When relevant for your country, you can add extra procedures where needed.

The quality of the Closing shows from the conclusion in the Control Files. It is important that the FD reviews the books and closing, and shares with us by email

- Control File
- Trial Balance / Management Hierarchy
- RMA file (Financial Performance Review)

The Control File is either clean, or issues are flagged and explained. Remediation should occur soon.
