> Reply by Egbert van Zomeren (8 Apr 2025) revising the FY2024 bonus true-up instruction in Ahmet Gunduz's mail of 27 Mar 2025; the quoted March mail is kept below because the revision refers to it ("in contrary to the below"). Inline images belong to the quoted mail.

Dear all,

Below is a revision to the Closing and Accounting instructions for Q1,

In contrary to the below, in order to make the Bonus Cost’24 comparable to the Bonus Costs / Budget’25, can you please

- Do **not** process the variance between the Bonus Accrual at yearend’24 and the Actual bonuses in the SCOA Accrual Releases line. But instead,
- Adjust your 2024 Bonus Accrual per the 2024 P&L (SCOA 27526) in such a way that is mirrors the actual bonus costs paid in March

Example

- Bonus accrual at yearend’24 was €16,800
- Actual bonus paid during March is €17,500
- Then ask FAM to open period 12/2024, and book in 12/2024 €700 as Debit 44124, Credit 27526. We do **not** book as Accrual Releases
- In period 03/2025 you offset the actual amount paid with the available bonus as Debit 27526 €17,500, Credit Bank

Let me know in case of questions,

Many thanks

Egbert

**Subject:** Closing and reporting instructions / 2025 March

Dear all,

Herewith are the instructions for the March 2025 closing of the Multi Asset, Legacy, and Service companies. The **18th of April 2025** is the deadline for March 2024 closing. Considering that you may have different deadlines for 3rd party mandates, our deadlines might be treated as guidance for the 3rd party asset companies, as it will enable you to close the Service companies properly. For accurate reporting and an efficient process, countries must complete the tasks below by the deadline.

**If you anticipate any delays in completing the closing, please let us know as soon as practical.**

To enhance efficiency, please use the latest shared Financial Performance Review (FPR) file to explain any material variances between actuals and budget. This proactive approach will help us avoid the need for follow-up calls to discuss budget differences.

We would like to ask your special attention to the items below besides the task list below.

- Bonus accruals for 2025Q1 will be communicated in a separate mail as the 2025 budget has not been approved yet.
- Please pay attention to control files to clean all issues before the year-end close.
- Please also try to settle (in cash) intercompany positions as much as possible before Quarter end.
- As you may have an unavoidable difference between the bonus actually paid for FY2024 at 2025 vs what has been accrued, please follow the below example to book the delta.

Example

Bonus accrual at December ’24 €100k

Bonus paid in March 2025 €120k

The expected booking is as follows

43010 Accrual release Debit €20k

27526 Bonus accrual Debit €100k

13311 Salary bank account Credit €120k

The** quality of closing procedures is **evidenced by the status of the control files. The FD review of control files and trial balances, evidenced by sign-off by the FD, is to ensure that nothing has come to the FDs attention that causes him to believe that the financials do not give a true and fair view of the financial position and results for the period. This procedure ensures that control files are either clean or that issues have been flagged and explained and will be resolved as soon as practical.
