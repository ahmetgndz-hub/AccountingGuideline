Dear all,

Herewith are the instructions for the February closing of the Multi Service, Legacy, and possibly Asset companies; considering that you may have different deadlines for 3rd party mandates, our deadlines might be treated as guidance for the 3rd party asset companies, as it will enable you to close the Service companies properly.

For accurate reporting and an efficient process, countries must complete the below tasks within the deadline (earlier is, of course, preferred).

For February 2025 closing the deadlineis set at** 13 March 2025**! Access to Coda will be closed centrally on the 14th of March. If you anticipate any delays in completing the closing, please let us know as soon as possible.

The** quality of closing **is evidenced by the status of the control files. The FD review of control files and trial balances, evidenced by FD sign-offs, is to ensure that nothing has come to the FDs attention that causes him to believe that the financials do not give a true and fair view of the financial position and results for the period. This procedure is to ensure that control files are either clean or that issues have been flagged and explained and will be resolved as soon as practical.
