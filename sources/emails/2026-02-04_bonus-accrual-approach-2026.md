Following the closing communication sent yesterday, I would like to clarify the approach for booking the bonus accruals for 2026.

For 2026, the total bonus amount relating to **FY2025**, i.e., the bonus approved to be paid to employees should be **accrued on a straight‑line basis over 12 months**.

Please ensure this methodology is applied consistently across all countries throughout 2026.
