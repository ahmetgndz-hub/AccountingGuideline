Dear all,

Below are the instructions for the **December 2025 Closing** of the Multi service and asset companies, and 3rd party asset companies. The deadline for the Closing is **Wednesday 21 January**

You will see the deadline is one week longer than a ‘normal’ monthly closing, given the importance of a yearend close. Please respect the timeline so we can do an accurate and timely communication of the 2025 results to the Board.

**Planning**

Critical steps are flagged below – please respect these timelines as we report to the Board on these aspects by end of the respective day

The December books in Coda will be closed on Thursday, 22 January. If you anticipate any delays in completion, let us know in advance.

**Closing Steps**

The minimal set of closing procedures is listed below and in the Closing Checklist on intranet. When relevant for your country, you can add extra procedures where needed.

Overall quality of the books shows from the Control File. The Finance Director reviews the books and closing, and shares the following with us by email:

- **Control File**

The Control File should be clean, without exceptions. A Control File that is not clean, is not accepted and means your Closing is not finalized. As a result, it holds up our reporting of the monthly results to the Board

- **Trial Balance / Management Hierarchy**
- **FPR file (Financial Performance Review)Attention items**

It goes without saying that it’s vital to be up to date in charging your 2025 fee income to our clients, and if not able to charge yet – accrue for the income.

Also, no suspense accounts, no ‘dummy’ positions, and be accurate in your cost accruals. When recharging to another Multi service company, communicate on timely basis

We will compare the actual 2025 Genex and CAF per country versus the Advances sent in 2025, and reconcile the variance with you. Please do the same for your Time recharges between countries, and communicate clearly with your counterpart to prevent IC differences,

Let me or Ahmet know in case of questions. Good luck in your yearend closing!
