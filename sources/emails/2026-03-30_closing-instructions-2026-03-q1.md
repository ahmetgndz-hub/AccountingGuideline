Dear all,

Below you will find the closing instructions and timeline for the Q1 / March 2026 closing of the Multi Service companies and Multi / 3rd Party owned Asset companies.
The deadlines outlined below are hard deadlines and must be respected.
They are required to ensure a timely, accurate and well‑controlled close and to allow proper reporting of Q1 actuals and updated forecasts.
Any expected delay or inability to meet these deadlines must be escalated immediately.

**Planning & Deadlines**

- Operational closing activities: up to 18 April 2026
- Final review, corrections and sign‑off: 17 April 2026
- Sharing of FPR (Financial Performance Review): 18 April 2026
Access to systems and reporting will be managed in line with this timeline.
Late bookings or late submissions after sign‑off will not be accepted.

**Non‑Negotiable Closing Requirements**
To be absolutely clear, the following expectations apply to all countries and entities:
1. Timely Closing

- All closing steps must be completed within the agreed timeline
- No open items should remain without explicit explanation and ownership
- Delays must be communicated before, not after, deadlines
2. Clean Control File

- The Control File must be clean at sign‑off
- Any remaining issues must be:

  - Clearly identified
  - Properly explained
  - Actively remediated

- A Control File with unexplained issues will not be considered acceptable
3. Forecast Requirement

- A forecast must be prepared and uploaded into CODA
- Where material deviations versus Budget exist, an updated forecast is mandatory
- Forecasts must reflect realistic assumptions and latest available information
- Missing or outdated forecasts will be treated as incomplete closing
4. Finance Director Accountability

- The Director of Finance review and sign‑off is required
- Sign‑off confirms:

  - Completeness of the closing
  - Accuracy of the numbers
  - Quality of the Control File

**Minimum Mandatory Deliverables**
The following documents are required for Q1 closing:

- Control File
- Trial Balance / Management Hierarchy
- RMA file (Financial Performance Review)
The quality of the closing will be assessed primarily through the Control File conclusions.
A clean and well‑supported Control File is therefore essential.

RMA Reporting
Further instructions regarding RMA reporting to the Board will be communicated separately.

Should you have questions or foresee any risk to timely completion, contact us immediately.
Kind regards,
Egbert van Zomeren
