Budget 2027 – Macroeconomic Assumptions Instruction
Inflation and FX rates for the budget period October 2026 – December 2027 | All operating countries, Service Companies and owned assets | Data cut-off 14 September 2026 | Version 1.0 – approved by the Board

1. Scope and status
This instruction sets the mandatory inflation and FX assumptions for Budget 2027 (Q4 2026 forecast and FY 2027) so that all country budgets are comparable and consolidate consistently in EUR. The assumption set has been approved by the Board and is frozen for the Budget 2027 round; it forms part of the Budget 2027 instructions and applies to both the local-currency and the EUR versions of the budget of every operating country, Service Company and owned or partly owned asset in scope. Only official sources are used (ECB, European Commission, IMF, OECD, national central banks and government programmes); source numbers refer to the list in Section 4. Apply the rates exactly as stated and confirm this in your budget narrative.

4. How to apply the assumptions
- Mandatory rates. Do not substitute local forecasts. If you consider a deviation necessary for a specific line, budget at the Group rate and list the alternative, quantified, as an item requiring management decision. Hold all rates in a single "Assumptions" sheet of your budget model so that a Group revision flows through without re-keying.
- FX translation. Q4 2026 forecast P&L and cash flow at the Q4 2026 average rate; FY 2027 P&L and cash flow at the quarterly average rates (or the FY 2027 average where the model is annual); balance sheet items and the Cash BoP → EoP reconciliation at the 31 Dec 2026 and 31 Dec 2027 closing rates.
- Rent indexation and index-linked fees. Apply the contractual index, reference month and indexation date, using actual published index values. Most 2027 indexation references 2026 index values, so the effective eurozone rate will be around the FY 2026 figure (about 3%; France 2,4% to Slovakia 4,3%) – do not apply the 2027 rate to 2027 indexation. Reflect caps, collars and waivers at tenant or unit level.
