Dear all,

Below are the instructions for the **August 2025 Closing** of the Multi service and asset companies, and 3rd party asset companies. The deadline for the Closing is **18th of September**.

Knowing you have various deadlines for 3rd party reporting, our internal timeline is a guidance to enable you to close your service companies. Please respect the timeline so we can do an accurate and timely communication of the August results to the Board.

**Planning**

While individual tasks have their own deadlines as per the schedule, the final deadline for the completion of the August 2025 closing is Thursday, 18 September.

Access to the August books in Coda will be closed on Friday, 19 September. If you anticipate any delays in completion, let us know in advance.

**Closing Steps**

The minimal set of closing procedures is listed in the attached schedule. When relevant for your country, you can add extra procedures where needed.

The quality of the closing shows from the conclusion in the Control Files. It is important that the Finance Director (FD) reviews the books and closing, and shares the following with us by email:

- **Control File**

The Control File should be clean, without exceptions. A Control File that is not clean, is not accepted and means your Closing is not finalized. As a result, it holds up our reporting of the August numbers to the Board

- **Trial Balance / Management Hierarchy **

- **FPR file (Financial Performance Review)Other items / service company forecast**
