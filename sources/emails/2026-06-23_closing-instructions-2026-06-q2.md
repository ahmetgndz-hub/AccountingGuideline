Dear Country Management Teams
As we approach the June / Q2 2026 closing cycle, we are introducing a few process updates to improve efficiency, strengthen alignment between country teams and HQ, and ensure that the Board presentations are consistent and of high quality.
Please review the unified guidelines, standardized closing steps, and the detailed timeline below.
**1. Key Deadlines & System Access**

- **Final Closing & Sign-off Deadline:** 17 July 2026

- **CODA System Closure:** Central access to CODA will be closed on **20 July 2026**.
If you anticipate any delays or challenges in meeting these timelines, please notify the HQ Finance team immediately.

**2. Standardized Closing Steps & Timeline**
The detailed execution schedule for the June 2026 closing is outlined below. While this represents the minimum required procedures, you may add country-specific steps if relevant to your local operations.

**3. Financial Deliverables & Sign-off Requirements**
The quality of our closing process is directly reflected in the completeness of the Control Files. The Finance Director (FD) of each country is required to thoroughly review the books and share the following final documents with HQ via email by **17 July 2026**:

- **Submit Control File:** Must either be completely clean, or any outstanding issues must be clearly flagged with an explicit remediation plan.

- **Sign-off of the Trial Balances (TB-Management Hierarchy)**

- **Submit the Financial Performance Review (FPR)4. Updates to RMA Reporting & Forecasting Process**
To streamline consolidation and enhance group transparency, please strictly adhere to the following updated guidelines:

- **12-Month Rolling Forecast:** At each quarter-end, all countries must provide an updated forecast for the upcoming 12 months. This data must be fully uploaded into CODA by **13 July 2026**, prior to our scheduled country-HQ review meetings.

- **Intercompany Charges Alignment:** To proactively reconcile intercompany expectations at the forecast stage, each country is now requested to fill out the shared form detailing planned charges to other group companies. Please present this in a **dedicated tab** within your RMA report.

- **Standardized Reporting Format:** We are shifting to a standardized RMA format integrated with Macabacus reporting to save preparation time. The updated template and delivery instructions will be shared shortly.

- **Review Meetings & Post-Meeting Adjustments:** Gerdien will reach out to schedule our joint country-HQ RMA review meetings over the two weeks following submission. Any adjustments agreed upon during these meetings must be integrated promptly. Updated presentations and final forecasts must be redistributed and uploaded to CODA within **5 business days** following your specific meeting.

**Note on 3rd Party Reporting:** Knowing you may have different deadlines for your 3rd Party reporting, our group deadlines can be treated as guidance for those entities, as you will need the data to properly close your Service companies. Further guidance on QAR (Multi-owned assets) and Board reporting will follow shortly.
Thank you all in advance for your cooperation and commitment to making this closing cycle smooth, transparent, and impactful for the Board.
Should you have any questions or require support from HQ during your preparations, please do not hesitate to reach out.
