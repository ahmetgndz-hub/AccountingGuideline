Dear all,

Please find below the closing instructions and timeline for the May 2026 closing of the Multi Service companies and Multi / 3rd Party owned Asset companies.

The deadlines outlined below must be respected. They are essential to ensure accurate and timely reporting of the May results. Any anticipated delay must be communicated in advance — not after a deadline has passed.

**Planning & Deadlines**

The detailed timetable is attached. In summary:

 • Operational closing : till 15 June 2026
 • Final review and sign-off : 15 June 2026
 • Sharing FPR file : 16 June 2026

Access to period May 2026 in CODA will be managed in line with this timeline. As a general principle, late adjustments will not be accepted once results have been communicated.

Key Closing Milestones

For reference, the main deadlines from the timetable are:

 • 26 May – Issuing Asset invoices (doccode = SI%, el4: PB, PC2%, PD%, PE1)
 • 02 Jun – Bank and Cash booking deadline (Map Code = BB5)
 • 04 Jun – FX valuation – Cash Items (Map Code = BB5)
 • 04 Jun – Last day of A/R review and matching (13111, 13130)
 • 04 Jun – Bad debt booking (13113, 42593, 43001)
 • 05 Jun – Salary and employee-related costs booking (Map Code = PQ*, el4 = 27515)
 • 05 Jun – Booking of depreciation (11313, 11323, 11423, 42911, 53910, 44910)
 • 05 Jun – Recharges IC and 3rd party (13553, 13554)
 • 08 Jun – Last day of Intercompany Transactions booking (el6 = R*)
 • 08 Jun – Last day of Invoice Bookkeeping (Document Code = *PI*)
 • 08 Jun – Fee Income at the Management company (Map Code = PP*)
 • 09 Jun – Property valuation bookkeeping (BA2, PK1)
 • 10 Jun – All accruals: Rent, MSC, Genex, Opex, FinEX, TO Rent, Bonus
 • 15 Jun – Review of Control File and TB (by Director of Finance)
 • 15 Jun – Last day of Corrections (Map Code = ALL)
 • 15 Jun – FX valuation, signing off clean control file and TB (by Director of Finance)
 • 16 Jun – Sharing FPR file (by Director of Finance)

**Process Steps1. Timely Closing**
All closing steps must be completed within the set timelines. No open items should remain without explicit explanation and clear ownership. Delays must be communicated proactively — before a deadline, not after.

**2. Clean Control File**
The Control File must be clean at sign-off. Any remaining issues must be (i) clearly identified, (ii) properly explained, and (iii) actively remediated. A Control File with unexplained issues will not be considered acceptable.

**3. Role of the Finance Director**
Active involvement of the Finance Director is essential throughout this process. The FD reviews and signs off on (i) completeness of the closing, (ii) accuracy of the numbers, and (iii) quality of the Control File.

**Deliverables**
 • Control File
 • Trial Balance / Management Hierarchy
 • FPR file (Financial Performance Review)
