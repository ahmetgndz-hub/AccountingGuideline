Dear all,

Below you will find the closing instructions and timeline for the April 2026 closing of the Multi Service companies and Multi / 3rd Party owned Asset companies.

The deadlines outlined below should be respected. They are required to ensure an accurate and timely reporting of the April results. Any expected delay should be communicated in advance.

**Planning & Deadlines**

The detailed timetable is attached, and in summary as follows:

 • Operational closing : till 16 May 2026
 • Final review and sign‑off : 18 May 2026
 • Sharing FPR file : 19 May 2026

Access to the period April 2026 in CODA will be managed in line with this timeline. As a general principle, late adjustments will not be accepted once results have been communicated.

**Key Closing Milestones**

For reference, the main deadlines from the timetable are:

 • 05 May – Bank and Cash booking deadline (Map Code = BB5)
 • 07 May – Last day of A/R review and matching
 • 08 May – Salary, depreciation and recharges booking
 • 11 May – Last day of Invoice Bookkeeping (Document Code = *PI*)
 • 11 May – Last day of Intercompany Transactions booking
 • 12 May – Property valuation bookkeeping
 • 13 May – All accruals (Rent, MSC, Genex, Opex, FinEX, TO rent, Bonus)
 • 18 May – Review of Control File and TB (by Director of Finance)
 • 18 May – Last day of Corrections (Map Code = ALL)
 • 18 May – FX valuation, signing off clean control file and TB (by Director of Finance)
 • 19 May – Sharing FPR file (by Director of Finance)

**Process Steps1. Timely Closing**
All closing steps should be completed within the set timelines. No open items should remain without explicit explanation and ownership. Delays must be communicated before, not after, deadlines.

**2. Clean Control File**
The Control File must be clean at sign‑off.
Any remaining issues must be
 (i) clearly identified,
 (ii) properly explained, and
 (iii) actively remediated.
Control File with unexplained issues will not be considered acceptable.

**3. Role of the Finance Director**
Active involvement of the Finance Director is pivotal to the process. The FD reviews and signs off on (i) completeness of the closing, (ii) accuracy of the numbers, and (iii) quality of the Control File.

**Deliverables**

 • Control File
 • Trial Balance / Management Hierarchy
 • RMA file (Financial Performance Review)

Please do not hesitate to reach out in case of any questions or concerns — happy to jump on a call.
