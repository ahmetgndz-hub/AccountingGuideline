Herewith are the instructions for the **July 2025 month-end closing** for the Multi Service and multi-third-party-owned Asset companies. According to the attached schedule, the closing deadline is **15th of August**.

Knowing you may have different deadlines for 3rd party reporting, our deadlines can be treated as guidance, as it will enable you to close your Service companies. The planning should be respected so we can do a proper and timely communication of the July results to the Board.

**Planning**

While individual tasks have their own deadlines as per the schedule, the final deadline for the completion of the July 2025 closing is Friday, 15 August 2025.

Access to Coda will be closed centrally on Saturday, 16 August 2025. If you anticipate any delays in completion, please let us know as soon as possible.

The minimal set of closing procedures is listed in the attached schedule. When relevant for your country, you can add extra procedures where needed.

**Control FileTrial Balance / Management HierarchyFPR file (Financial Performance Review)**
