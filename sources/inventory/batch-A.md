# Batch A — closing instructions 2024-10 … 2025-06 (intake 2026-10-09)

Nine Outlook messages, converted with `tools/mail_html_to_md.py` into `sources/emails/`.

General notes for the whole batch:

- In every mail the **Key Closing Milestones timetable is an inline PNG image**, not HTML. The
  converter therefore produces no table; the rows below were transcribed by reading the inline
  image through the Microsoft 365 connector (`read_resource` on the attachment URI). The
  control-file performance map (coloured Europe map) is also an inline image and was not
  transcribed. Nothing was downloaded; no non-inline attachments exist in this batch.
- Column header of the timetable in all mails: *Process | Date | Day | Groups will be closed /
  followed up*. The Nov 2024 timetable has an extra *when* column (working days relative to
  month end, "ME ± n"). Rows marked with a red flag in the image are the hard milestones; they
  are marked **(milestone)** below.
- Converter artefact (not hand-edited, cosmetic only): bold runs that start with a space in
  Word come out as `The** quality of closing procedures is **evidenced` and
  `timelineis set at**18 July 2025.**`. The text is complete.
- The standard timetable rows and their codes are identical from month to month; they are
  repeated in full for each mail so every file can be read on its own.
