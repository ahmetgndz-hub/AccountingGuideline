# Business glossary and KPIs

<div class="page-meta" markdown>
**Applies to:** all · **Owner:** Group Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08
</div>

Definitions of the operational terms used in Multi reporting. Mall income (kiosks, temporary stands, ATMs, antennas, temporary storage) is excluded from every sqm-based KPI.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Gross rental income (GRI)** | All rental income accrued from tenants in the period: base rent, turnover rent, parking, mall income and other asset income, after vacancy and discounts. Equals the cash received. See [Rental income structure](revenue-structure.md). |
| **Net rental income (NRI)** | All revenue from the asset(s) less operating expenses, including service and marketing costs allocated to tenants. Includes base rent, parking, turnover rent and service / marketing charges. Before tax, amortisation, interest and financing costs, depreciation, valuation results, general expenses and income tax. Reporting groups PA to PG excluding PD5A and PGH. |
| **Mall income** | Income from renting space in the common areas: kiosks, temporary stands, ATMs, antennas, temporary storage. |
| **Vacancy** | A unit not occupied or otherwise in use that can be leased to another tenant. See [ERV and vacancy](erv-vacancy.md). |
| **Physical occupancy** | Leased sqm / leasable sqm. Includes long-term (over one year) storage linked to a lease; excludes mall income units. |
| **Financial occupancy** | Average occupancy over the year on the basis of ERV at the reporting date, as a percentage of theoretical rental revenue; vacant units valued at ERV. Includes long-term storage, excludes mall income units. |
| **Annual sales per sqm** | Total annual tenant sales (excluding VAT) / GLA, excluding storage and mall income units. |
| **Like-for-like sales per sqm (trailing 12 months)** | Same, restricted to comparable sales-reporting tenants (reported sales for the last 24 consecutive months), excluding storage in the denominator. |
| **OCR (occupancy cost ratio, effort index)** | Trailing 12 months: tenant occupancy cost (rent, service and marketing charges, after discounts) / tenant sales. Only tenants with at least 12 consecutive months of sales; excludes non-sales-reporting tenants, shop consumption utilities, special units (petrol stations) and mall income. |
| **Footfall** | Number of people entering the property or unit in a period. |
| **Collection rate** | Amounts invoiced in the last 12 months and recovered against those same invoices / total amounts invoiced in that period. Accrual basis, current leases only. Depends on correct [matching](matching.md): overpayments do not count. |
| **Debtor exposure** | Total debt of the tenant − received guarantees (security level 1 and 2). |
| **ERV** | Estimated rental value, the market rent of the unit as stored in Horizon. |
| **PBR** | Potential base rent: the full contract rent excluding incentives, discounts and vacancy. |
