# Budget and forecast instructions

<div class="page-meta" markdown>
**Applies to:** service companies, Multi-owned asset companies (UA, IT, NL) and all entities for tax and cash · **Owner:** Group Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-09
</div>

## Rule

1. **Quarterly forecast.** At each quarter end every country uploads an updated **12-month rolling forecast** to Coda by the date in the closing instruction (Q2 2026: 13 July, before the country–HQ review meetings). Agreed adjustments from the review meeting are integrated, and the updated presentation and final forecast are redistributed and re-uploaded to Coda **within five business days** after the meeting.
2. **Annual budget.** The budget cycle is part of the Q3 close: accounting close on the Q3 deadline (2026: 16 October), budget presentations at the RMA meeting about two weeks later. The instruction of 10 September 2026 (*Budget 2027 – Instructions and Guidelines*) applies to all countries and entities in scope and replaces every earlier budget guidance.
3. **Upload codes and currencies.** Budget uploads use balance code **BUD**, forecasts the FCST code. EUR and local currency are uploaded as **two separate submissions with identical coding**. Aggregated uploads and uploads missing a dimension are rejected. Do not wait for Board approval to upload: consolidation needs the numbers while they are under discussion.
4. **HQ assumptions are mandatory.** Inflation (CPI), FX rates, CAF per quarter and redundancy cost are issued centrally and applied exactly as stated; a country that disagrees budgets at the Group rate and lists its alternative as an item for management decision.
5. **Intercompany recharges must net out.** Use the central IC Charges file; HQ confirms when cross-country charges are final. The IC recharge check covers (i) direct cost recharges, (ii) time recharges between countries and (iii) HQ / CAF time recharges. At quarter end, planned charges to other group companies go into the dedicated intercompany tab of the RMA report.
6. **Closing comes first.** The closing deadlines take priority over forecast and budget work: the actuals are the starting point of the forecast, and the closing gates the RMA submission and the Budget presentation.

## Budget 2027 instruction (10 September 2026)

**Structure.** Part A service companies (entities employing staff and managing assets for property owners). Part B owned assets (Ukraine, Italy, Netherlands; full shopping-centre budget, partly owned assets on a 100% basis). Part C all entities: corporate income tax and cash reconciliation.

**Every submission** uses the RMA presentation template issued for the cycle (earlier versions are rejected), is delivered in EUR and local currency as two uploads with identical coding, and respects the minimum Coda coding levels below.

**Reporting periods:** Actuals 2025 full year; Budget 2026 full year as approved; Forecast 2026 Q4 only (Q1 to Q3 2026 actuals are not repeated); Budget 2027 by quarter.

### Part A: service companies

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Fee income basis | Derived directly from the approved budget of the portfolio Multi manages, strictly per the signed management agreements: each fee at its contractual definition, rate, base, cap or floor and billing frequency. Blended or rule-of-thumb assumptions are not acceptable. Drivers (NOI, rental income, service charges, collections, capex, leasing transactions) are identical to the approved asset budget so that fee income and the asset's fee expense reconcile. |
| Fee detail | Budgeted per fee type (EL4 + EL5). Upload level EL1 + EL2 (asset) + EL4 + EL5, no counterparty on fee lines. Categories PP1 asset management, PP2 property management, PP3 statutory services, PP4 (re)development, PP5 salary and employee recharges, PP6 other recharges, PP7 fund management fees. |
| New business, disposals | New business only where the agreement is signed. No asset disposal anticipated unless the owner granted exclusivity. Leasing fee assumptions outlined: frictional vacancy, active vacancy management, structural vacancy. |
| Staff cost | Managed separately between country MDs and the Multi Board; salaries and bonuses proposed to the Board must match the submission exactly; salary levels as they are plus CPI. FTE schedule split direct / indirect for actual Q3 2026, year-end 2026 and year-end 2027. New hires only when Board-approved or pre-approved and funded by a third-party owner. Redundancy cost is provided by HQ. Staff recharges to assets wherever the management contract permits. |
| Gross margin | Fee income − direct staff cost − allocated indirect staff cost and genex; indirect cost allocated to mandates with the standard key (average lease count relative to the total portfolio). |

### Part B: owned assets

Rental income at tenant and unit level from the contracted rent roll; ERV per unit re-assessed; indexation per lease clause with the HQ CPI; turnover rent separately from projected tenant sales; 2027 expiries and break options with renewal probability, ERV and downtime; incentives (rent-free, temporary discounts, fit-out contributions) shown separately. Service charge budget per asset with recoverable income versus recoverable cost and the owner's share (voids, caps, leakage) with the assumed recovery rate; other income (parking, specialty leasing, media, other) separately. Opex at asset level, split recoverable / non-recoverable by category, consistent with the service charge budget; management fees charged to the asset reconcile with Part A. Capex per project with description, total cost, quarterly phasing, split maintenance / development / leasing capex and status committed / approved / proposed, consistent with the cash reconciliation.

### Part C: tax and cash

Current tax budgeted on the projected taxable result at the applicable rate, with the bridge from accounting to taxable result (permanent and temporary differences, tax losses carried forward) and cash tax payments by quarter. Cash reconciliation per entity and quarter: cash BoP (year-end 2026 forecast balance) + net result + working capital movements (receivables, payables, accruals, intercompany, with commentary) − cash taxes − capex and other investments ± other items = cash EoP, tying to the budget balance sheet.

### Minimum Coda coding for the upload

| Line | Coding |
|---|---|
| Fee income PP1 to PP4, PP6 | EL1 + EL2 + EL4 + EL5 (EL4 + EL5 combination unique, no EL6) |
| Salary and employee recharges PP5, time charges PQ12, cost recharged from / to group PR14, HQ time charges PW / PWe | EL1 + EL2 + EL4 + EL6 counterparty, agreeing with the central IC Charges workbook |
| Staff costs PQ1, PQ2, PQ3, PQ7, PQ8, PQ10, PQ11 | EL1 + EL2 + EL4 (SCoA level, never per employee) |
| General costs PR1 to PR13, non-recurring PT (per item, with explanation) | EL1 + EL2 + EL4 + EL5 |
| Accrual releases and depreciation PU, financial result PV, corporate income tax PX (current and deferred separately) | EL1 + EL2 + EL4 |
| Owned assets: rental income PA, PB and vacancy, discounts, rent-free PC1, PC2T, PC2B, other rent PD | EL1 + EL2 + EL4 + EL6 tenant where attributable |
| Owned assets: service and marketing charge income PE1, PE2 | EL1 + EL2 + EL4 |
| Owned assets: service charge costs PF1 to PF16, opex PG1 to PG4X | EL1 + EL2 + EL4 + EL5 (recoverable / non-recoverable split) |
| Capex | EL1 + EL2 + EL4 + EL5 + capex id (linked to the Reporting Hub) |
| Balance sheet, working capital and cash lines | EL1 + EL2 + EL4 + EL5 |

Lines not listed may be uploaded at EL1 + EL4. The forecasting file holds the build-up (per employee, per tenant); the Coda upload holds the minimum coding, which is the level reported and compared with actuals.
