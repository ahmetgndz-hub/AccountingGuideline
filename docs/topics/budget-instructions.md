# Budget and forecast instructions

<div class="page-meta" markdown>
**Applies to:** service companies and Multi-owned asset companies · **Owner:** Group Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-09
</div>

## Rule

1. **Quarterly forecast.** At each quarter end every country uploads an updated **12-month rolling forecast** to Coda by the date in the closing instruction (Q2 2026: 13 July, before the country–HQ review meetings). The forecast is reviewed in a meeting with HQ; agreed adjustments are integrated and the updated presentation and final forecast are redistributed and re-uploaded to Coda **within five business days** after the meeting.
2. **Upload codes.** Budget uploads use balance code **BUD**; forecast uploads the FCST code. Re-upload after HQ shares updated genex and CAF figures.
3. **Do not wait for Board approval** to upload: the forecast and budget numbers are needed for consolidation even while they are under discussion.
4. **Intercompany recharges must net out.** With the budget, complete the IC recharge check covering (i) direct cost recharges, (ii) time recharges between countries and (iii) HQ / CAF time recharges. At quarter end, list the charges planned to other group companies in the dedicated intercompany tab of the RMA report.
5. **Reporting format.** The RMA (Financial Performance Review) uses the standardised Macabacus-based format; see [Reports and available budget](reports-available-budget.md).
6. Closing comes first: the closing deadlines in the monthly instruction take priority, because the actuals are the starting point of the forecast and the closing gates the RMA submission and the Budget presentation.

The Budget 2027 instructions (service companies and portfolio fee income) are being added from the e-mail of 10 September 2026; see the change log.
