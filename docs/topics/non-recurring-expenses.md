# Non-recurring expenses

<div class="page-meta" markdown>
**Applies to:** all entities · **Owner:** Group Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08
</div>

## Rule

Management reporting splits recurring from non-recurring expenses. Book non-recurring items only on the accounts below (checklist point 33) so that no manual collection is needed.

| SCoA | Name | BO | Element 6 | Element 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 44125 | Redundancy (staff) | PQ5 | Employee | Department |
| 44126 | Staff cost (non-recurring) | PQ5 | Employee | Department |
| 44591 | Restructuring costs (general) | PR12 | Creditor / group / related | Department |
| 44594 | Advisory (non-recurring) | PR12 | Creditor / group / related | Department |
| 44596 | Other non-recurring cost | PR12 | Creditor / group / related | Department |

Income side: PD5A non-recurring income and PGH accrual releases (42551, 43000) are excluded from NRI.
