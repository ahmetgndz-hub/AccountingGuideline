Summaries of the group accounting memos that underlie the IFRS books. The full memos (and the IFRS 16 memos on office and car leases and lease incentives, 8 April 2021) are PDFs on the old wiki; ask Group Finance for the current versions.

## IFRS 15 Revenue from contracts with customers (applied 1 January 2018, cumulative effect method)

- **Rental income** (base rent, turnover rent, mall income) is lease income under IAS 17 / IFRS 16, recognised straight-line over the lease term; contingent rent (turnover rent) when it arises. Initial direct costs of a lease are expensed over the lease term. Discounts (operational support, rent-free) reduce rental revenue straight-line over the lease. Termination and dilapidation payments are recognised when the right to receive them arises.
- **Service charge income** is one performance obligation under IFRS 15, recognised **over time** on the output method, **gross**, with Multi as principal (Multi is responsible for the service and sets the price). Advances are invoiced monthly on budget and settled against actuals at year end; the budget figures are the key estimate.

## IFRS 9 Financial instruments (applied 1 January 2018, no restatement)

- Classification: amortised cost, FVOCI, FVTPL based on business model and cash flow characteristics; no significant change for liabilities.
- Impairment: expected credit loss (ECL). Trade receivables use the **simplified approach** (lifetime ECL) with a provision matrix built on three years of default history and age bands 0 to 30, 31 to 60 and over 60 days. Receivables over 60 days overdue, tenants under legal process and tenants that have left the centre are treated as default; the whole receivable of that tenant is impaired. This is consistent with the MAN [bad debt policy](bad-debt.md).
- Receivables from shareholders: parametric ECL using Moody's default and recovery rates for the ultimate parent's rating. Cash: 12-month ECL, low credit risk, not significant.
