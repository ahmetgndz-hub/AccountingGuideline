Summaries of the group accounting memos that underlie the IFRS books. ## IFRS 15 Revenue from contracts with customers (applied 1 January 2018, cumulative effect method)

- **Rental income** (base rent, turnover rent, mall income) is lease income under IAS 17 / IFRS 16, recognised straight-line over the lease term; contingent rent (turnover rent) when it arises. Initial direct costs of a lease are expensed over the lease term. Discounts (operational support, rent-free) reduce rental revenue straight-line over the lease. Termination and dilapidation payments are recognised when the right to receive them arises.
- **Service charge income** is one performance obligation under IFRS 15, recognised **over time** on the output method, **gross**, with Multi as principal (Multi is responsible for the service and sets the price). Advances are invoiced monthly on budget and settled against actuals at year end; the budget figures are the key estimate.

## IFRS 9 Financial instruments (applied 1 January 2018, no restatement)

- Classification: amortised cost, FVOCI, FVTPL based on business model and cash flow characteristics; no significant change for liabilities.
- Impairment: expected credit loss (ECL). Trade receivables use the **simplified approach** (lifetime ECL) with a provision matrix built on three years of default history and age bands 0 to 30, 31 to 60 and over 60 days. Receivables over 60 days overdue, tenants under legal process and tenants that have left the centre are treated as default; the whole receivable of that tenant is impaired. This is consistent with the MAN [bad debt policy](bad-debt.md).
- Receivables from shareholders: parametric ECL using Moody's default and recovery rates for the ultimate parent's rating. Cash: 12-month ECL, low credit risk, not significant.

## IFRS 16 Leases: office and car leases (memo V 08-04-2021)

**Scope at Multi:** office rent, car leases and office equipment leases (copiers, printers). The memo applies to the FSM / FSE books; MAN and UST keep leases as operating cost.

- **Recognition.** On commencement recognise a right-of-use (ROU) asset and a lease liability. ROU asset = lease liability plus initial direct costs, adjusted for incentives received, prepayments and restoration obligations; carried at cost less depreciation and impairment.
- **Lease liability** = net present value of the lease payments over the lease term, discounted at the rate implicit in the lease or, if not available, the incremental borrowing rate. Index- or rate-based variable payments are included at commencement-date values; other variable payments go to P&L when triggered. Remeasure when the lease term or a purchase-option assessment changes (revised discount rate) or when a residual value guarantee or an index changes (unchanged rate).
- **Lease term** = non-cancellable period plus extension periods that are reasonably certain to be used and termination options that are reasonably certain not to be exercised.
- **Exemptions.** Leases of 12 months or less without purchase option (election per asset class) and low-value assets (election per lease) may be expensed straight-line.
- **Multi policy choices.** No split of non-lease (service) components; lease term = contract term (no extension or purchase options assumed, no early-termination estimate when an employee leaves); ROU asset = total contract value, depreciated over the contract term; lease liability = remaining contract value.
- **Disclosure.** Movements of the lease asset and liability in the PP&E movement schedule, plus a Lease Arrangements note with opening and closing carrying value, depreciation, lease payments and interest charge.
