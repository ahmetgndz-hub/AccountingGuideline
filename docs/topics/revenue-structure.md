# Rental income structure

<div class="page-meta" markdown>
**Applies to:** asset companies · **Owner:** Group Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08
</div>

## Rule

Rental income is reported top-down from the market rent to the cash received, so that every gap is visible on its own line. The chain is the same in every case: **ERV → reversionary potential → PBR → vacancy and discounts → base rent (cash) → turnover rent, parking, mall income → GRI**.

| BO group | Line | Content | SCoA |
|---|---|---|---|
| PA | ERV | Estimated rental value, from Horizon, booked monthly at asset level | 41101 |
| PB | Reversionary potential | ERV reversal and potential base rent (PBR) | 41110, 41111, 41170 |
| PC1 | Vacancy | Gap between PBR and cash for empty units | 41130 |
| PC2 | Short-term discounts | Operational discounts, stepped rents, fit-out phase | 41410, 41411, 41420 |
| PC2A | Structural discounts | Permanent commercial price adjustments | 41412 |
| PC2B | Rent free | Rent-free periods | 41415 |
| PC2C | Turnover rent only | Negative or positive short-term discount for TO-rent-only tenants | 41416 |
| PD1 | Turnover rent | Rent above or instead of base rent, linked to tenant sales | 41140 |
| PD2 | Parking rent | | 41150 |
| PD3 | Mall income | Kiosks, temporary stands, ATMs, antennas, temporary storage | 41160 |
| PD5 | Other income | Other asset income | 41530 |

Rules of thumb:

- **PBR equals the full contract rent**, excluding stepped rents, rent-free periods, operational discounts and vacancy. Never adjust ERV or PBR because of a discount or a vacancy.
- **Base rent equals the cash received.** GRI equals the actual cash received including turnover rent and mall income.
- The difference between PBR and cash is shown as **vacancy** (unit empty) or **discount** (unit occupied but paying less).
