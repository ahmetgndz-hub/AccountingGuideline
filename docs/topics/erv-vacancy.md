# ERV and vacancy

<div class="page-meta" markdown>
**Applies to:** asset companies · **Owner:** Group Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08
</div>

## Rule

**ERV** is booked monthly at asset level with `JV-ERV` (41101 ERV, 41111 ERV reversal, 41416 TO rent only), so that reversionary potential is presented correctly and the books agree with Horizon and the budget. Control C20 compares full-year ERV in Horizon (A) with the budget (B), the YTD bookings (D ERV, E reversal, F TO rent only) and the expected YTD amounts (G = A/12 × period, H = B/12 × period). Only D + E + F may differ from zero by the agreed rent minus ERV.

**Vacancy**: a unit is vacant when it is not occupied or otherwise in use **and** can be leased to another tenant. When the unit is in use but pays less or nothing, that is a [discount](discounts-incentives.md), not vacancy.

- When a unit is vacant, report it as vacancy on 41130 and nowhere else.
- Do not reverse ERV or PBR to zero. PBR equals the full contract rent; the gap between PBR and cash is vacancy; base rent equals the cash received.
- Change PBR (via the reversionary potential line) only from the start date of a new lease. A lease signed in Q1 that starts in Q3 does not change PBR in Q2: report vacancy equal to ERV, because a temporary tenant may still use the unit.
- Deliberately keeping a unit empty (for example to combine it with the neighbouring unit) is still vacancy.
