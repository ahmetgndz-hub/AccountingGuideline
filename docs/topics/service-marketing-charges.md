# Service and marketing charges

<div class="page-meta" markdown>
**Applies to:** asset companies · **Owner:** Group Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08
</div>

## Rule

Recoverable costs are booked in two BO groups: **PE** service and marketing charge income (advances invoiced to tenants; 46810 service income, 46820 marketing income) and **PF** service and marketing charge expenses (46921 to 46937). Lease clauses change what the tenant pays: **cap** (tenant share limited to a maximum, the rest is landlord cost), **fixed** (tenant pays a fixed amount, gain or loss stays with the landlord), **floor** (minimum contribution, usually combined with a cap).

At least at every quarter end, reconcile actual cost against tenant contributions and book:

| Situation | Example | Booking |
|---|---|---|
| A. Shortfall from caps | Actual share 100, capped at 80, shortfall 20 | No booking; the net of PE and PF shows it |
| B. Shortfall from vacancy | Vacant unit's share 100 | No booking; the net of PE and PF shows it |
| C. Additional invoice to be issued | Actual share 70, paid 60 | Dr 13553 accrued income / Cr PE income 10 |
| D. Credit note to be issued | Actual share 70, paid 80 | Dr PE income / Cr 27560 other payables 10 |
| E. Fixed or floor contribution above actual | Actual 70, paid 85 | No booking |

After these bookings the **net balance of PE and PF equals shortfall from caps plus shortfall from vacancy** (checklist point 25) and is disclosed on the service charge page of the QAR. Advances are reviewed against the last two months and charged sqm is reconciled with GLA (point 24).

Marketing invoices not yet issued are accrued the same way (see [Accruals](accruals.md)). Under IFRS 15 service charge income is a single performance obligation recognised over time, gross, with Multi as principal; see [IFRS memos](ifrs-memos.md).
