# US tax reporting (UST books)

<div class="page-meta" markdown>
**Applies to:** BX-owned asset companies · **Owner:** Group Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08
</div>

## Rule

- Buildings and improvements (BS-TANCO-BUIMP) are reported at **purchase price**, without adding capex.
- Capex incurred in the period is released to the valuation result (PL-DEPAM-REVAL) at the reporting date.
- Capex for (re)development is classified as "Buildings and improvements under construction" until completion.
- The reporting pack shows capex in a separate tab split between valuation effect and capex incurred, reconciled to the amount posted to the valuation result.
- No deferred tax in UST books (see [Taxes](taxes.md)).
