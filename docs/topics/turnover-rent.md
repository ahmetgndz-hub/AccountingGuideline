# Turnover rent

<div class="page-meta" markdown>
**Applies to:** asset companies · **Owner:** Group Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08
</div>

## Rule

Turnover rent is rent linked to the tenant's sales. Monthly tenant sales are uploaded to Horizon for OCR reporting, and every turnover rent amount is either invoiced or accrued at each close (checklist point 34, accrual Dr 13553 / Cr 41140).

| Case | Lease | Reporting | Booking |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Performance TO tenant** | Base rent plus a turnover percentage on top | PBR = contract base rent; base rent = cash; the excess is turnover rent (PD1) | 41140 Turnover rent |
| **2 Temporary TO rent only** | Lease has a base rent but Multi waived it for a period and the tenant pays turnover rent only | PBR unchanged; the waived base rent is a short-term discount; turnover received on 41140 | 41416 TO rent only (PC2C) for the discount, 41140 for the rent |
| **3 TO rent only from day 1 (full turnover contract, no minimum)** | Rent entirely a percentage of sales | PBR = ERV; base rent = 0; everything received is turnover rent; the gap PBR − turnover is a short-term discount that can be positive or negative depending on sales | 41140 and 41416 |
| **Partly turnover contract (with minimum rent)** | Percentage of sales with a guaranteed minimum | PBR = the fixed minimum; base rent = cash up to the minimum; the excess is turnover rent | 41140 |

Instruction "Turnover rent accounting and reporting" (5 March 2020) and the worked examples (P&L layouts for a five-unit asset) are in the old wiki images; the rules above are the policy.
