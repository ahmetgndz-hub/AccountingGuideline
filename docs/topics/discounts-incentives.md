# Discounts and lease incentives

<div class="page-meta" markdown>
**Applies to:** asset companies · **Owner:** Group Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08
</div>

## Rule

Classify every reduction against the contract rent in one of the categories below and record it in Horizon the same way (checklist point 13). In **MAN** the discount hits the P&L when the cash is lower; in **FSM / FSE** (IFRS) lease incentives are straight-lined over the lease term on **43400 IFRS rent straight-lining** (PK2). In all cases PBR stays at the full contract rent and base rent equals the cash received.

| Category | Definition | MAN account | IFRS treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stepped rent** | Rent agreed in the lease grows over several years (60, 70, 80 against ERV 100) | 41411 Stepped rents (PC2) | Straight-lined, 43400 |
| **Operational (temporary) discount** | Amendment lowering the rent for less than one year | 41410 Operational discounts (PC2) | Straight-lined, 43400 |
| **Rent-free period** | Period without rent agreed in the lease | 41415 Rent frees (PC2B) | Straight-lined, 43400 |
| **Structural discount** | Permanent decrease of the rent agreed during the contract. Not an incentive but a commercial price adjustment; ERV is not adjusted in the current period | 41412 Structural discounts (PC2A) | Same as MAN |
| **Fit-out phase discount** | Short-term compensation while part of the unit is not operational during the tenant's fit-out. Not an incentive, not capitalised | 41420 Fit-out phase (PC2) | Same as MAN |
| **Turnover rent only** | See [Turnover rent](turnover-rent.md) | 41416 (PC2C) | |

Tenant fit-out works and direct cash contributions are capex category C4, not discounts; see [Capex](capex.md). Discounts to tenants granted with back-dated effect are accrued on 27560 against 414xx (see [Accruals](accruals.md)).
